FAQ

Questions new editors ask

Q

Is zenky cut good for beginners?

Yes. It is beginner-friendly because it keeps key editing tools easy to find while still offering advanced motion and audio controls.

Q

Do I need advanced effects to make content stand out?

No. Better cuts, stronger pacing, cleaner sound, and readable captions usually matter more than flashy transitions.

Q

What should I create first?

Start with a short montage, travel clip, product video, or TikTok-style edit. Practice on small projects before tackling long-form edits.
